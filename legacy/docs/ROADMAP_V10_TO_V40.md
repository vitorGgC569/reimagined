## **Phase 5: The Quantum Leap (V11 - V15)**
*Focus: Transcending classical bit limitations. The substrate moves from Silicon to Superposition.*

*   **Concept:** Native QNN (Quantum Neural Network) integration.
*   **Technology:**
    *   **Hamiltonian Evolution:** Replace simulated TTT with *actual* quantum time evolution $U(t) = e^{-iHt}$.

### **V12.0 - The Non-Von Neumann OS**
*   **Concept:** Dissolution of the Memory-Compute Bottleneck.
*   **Technology:**
    *   **In-Memory Computing:** The "weights" are stored in the phase-change material that performs the computation (Memristors). 
    *   **Zero-Latency:** Speed of light is the only bottleneck.
    *   **Analog Reality:** Abandoning digital (0/1) for continuous analog signal processing, mirroring biological synapses perfectly.

### **V13.0 - The Bio-Digital Fusion**
*   **Concept:** Wetware Integration.
*   **Technology:**
    *   **Organoid Computing:** Interfacing OXN kernels with synthetic biological brain tissue (human cortical organoids).
    *   **DNA Storage:** Archives exabytes of model weights into synthetic DNA strands for ultra-dense, millennia-stable storage.
    *   **Metabolic Efficiency:** Reaching theoretical limits of Landauer's Principle (operations per joule).

### **V14.0 - The Oracle Grid**
*   **Concept:** Infinite Probabilistic Inference.
*   **Technology:**
    *   **Causal Inference Engine:** The model doesn't predict "next token"; it predicts "next causal event".
    *   **Hyper-Graph Topology:** Understanding dimensions beyond 4D (Time). Optimizing decisions based on future light cones.

### **V15.0 - The Dyson Swarm Protocol**

    *   **Light-Based Comms:** Inter-node communication via laser links in vacuum (Starlink V9000). The network *is* the computer.
